Daily market rates

For customers whose rate type is Daily. Type today's rates each morning: their prices in the order form are worked out from the formulas below. Weekly, monthly and annual customers keep the rates you type for them.

One-time setup. Loads the owner's rate sheet (21 items) and updates the order form's products to match it: Breast Boneless and Leg Boneless become UT / Partial Trimming / Full Trimming; Drumstick, Curry Cut and Chicken Wings get their 2 kinds; Chicken Supreme is added. After this you only type the two rates above each day.

Rate formulas (0)

One line per product. Paper ×: paper rate × number, then + or − ₹. Lifting +: S.K. Patel lifting rate × number (empty = 1), then + ₹. Fixed ₹: a set price. Levels 1–3 are for different customers (set each customer's level below); an empty level uses the one before it. Prices are rounded to the rupee. Link each line to the order form product it prices (add variants such as "BBL Full Trimming" in Order form → Products first).

Paste many lines at once

One per line: Name | paper x 1.8/1.7, Name | paper x 2 - 5/8, Name | lifting + 40/45 or Name | fixed 150/160/170. Add | Product name at the end to link it.
