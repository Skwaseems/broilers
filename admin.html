Catalogue on request

When a customer sends “Catalogue”, “Hi”, “Menu” or “Price list” to your business WhatsApp (+91 83695 78332), they automatically get the ticked items as tall photos, one per item, each with its details and an order link — at most once every 12 hours each. Because the customer messaged first, WhatsApp delivers it even when a sent-out catalogue would be held back, and doesn’t charge for it. They’re also added to Customers.
